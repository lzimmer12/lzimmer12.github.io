The benevolent Mary Church Terrel Library. Frequently, I fear that this tomb of intellectual purity is smeared by our ever-cynical populace. For it is drab and neutral; it is a space in which the world is no more that we have familiarized ourselves with, but rather a hole to throw your diminishing corneas into. In its mass of text, Mudd invites us to understand the curious unknowns, but its stupendous reach of information sneers at our relatively small memory. In relative infinities, Mudd (and for that matter every library) is a deity. Than this what could bring us more solace? We are provided a home to raise our children of thought.

One may disagree with the beauty, but the architecture irrevokably speaks to Mudd's presence and longevity. For all the time a person inhabits, the brutal, enveloping features of our library blink past. Concrete does not rip and tear, instead it holds.

Thus, as one looks upon our studies within Mudd, a uniquely personal experience is shared. If you have any desire to share evidence of this, the goal is for this site to prove suitable.
